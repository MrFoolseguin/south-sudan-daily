2. Dinka Heritage Lesson: The Social Structure of Age-Sets (Ric)

In Dinka (Muonyjang) society, social identity and civic responsibility outside the immediate clan are anchored in the age-set system, known as Ric (or Rik). When young men reach late adolescence, they undergo traditional initiation together, forming a named age-set that binds them as peers for life.

Members of a Ric share deep obligations of solidarity, mutual aid, and collective defense. They eat together, assist one another during economic hardship, and arbitrate disputes within their group. Historically, age-sets formed the primary structure for community defense and public works. For anyone looking to understand leadership and community dynamics in South Sudan today, recognizing the influence of Ric is essential, as respect, trust, and social influence frequently flow through these lifelong peer networks.
