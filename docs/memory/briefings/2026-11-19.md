## 2. Dinka Heritage Lesson: *Ric* (Age-Sets) and Social Structure
In Muonyjang culture, social organization and civic responsibility are structured around age-sets, known as *ric* (plural *riëc*). Young men of similar age cohorts are initiated together, marking their formal transition from boys (*dhou*) to adult men (*wut* or *muony*). Initiation historically involved forehead scarring (*nhiem*) and receiving a dedicated ox-name (*wööddhe*), establishing a lifelong bond of solidarity, mutual defense, and shared accountability among age-mates.

Members of a *ric* work together, share meals, and protect their community’s cattle camps (*wut*). Age-sets create a clear order of seniority across Dinka society. Decisions in the community rely heavily on consensus across these groups, where older age-sets provide judicial guidance and historical memory, while younger age-sets handle physical security and executive tasks. For anyone looking to understand community leadership and social trust among the Dinka, understanding the enduring authority of *ric* is essential.

## 3. Local Intelligence

### Government Opens 14 Oil Exploration Blocks
Caretaker Petroleum Minister Awow Daniel Chuang announced that South Sudan is offering 14 oil exploration blocks to investors, aiming to raise crude oil production past 200,000 barrels per day within two years *(Sudans Post)*. Concurrently, government officials announced plans to construct two domestic oil refineries to reduce dependence on imported refined fuel *(Eye Radio)*.
*Why it matters:* Rebuilding crude output and building local refining capacity are central to stabilizing state revenues, though success depends heavily on regional pipeline security and pipeline transit arrangements through Sudan.

### African Union Deploys Electoral Expert to Juba
An African Union election expert arrived in Juba on Wednesday to work directly with the National Elections Commission (NEC) as voter registration continues across the country *(Sudans Post, Eye Radio)*.
*Why it matters:* The AU’s technical involvement lends institutional backing to the NEC's revised February 2027 election timeline, even as opposition groups like the ANC continue to challenge the legal basis of the postponement.

### Elegu Border Post Reopens as Hydropower Feasibility Advances
The border post between South Sudan and Uganda at Elegu has officially reopened *(Eye Radio)*. Separately, South Sudan signed an agreement with the Nile Equatorial Lakes Subsidiary Action Program Coordination Unit (NELSAP-CU) to conduct studies for the Grand Fulla hydropower project *(Eye Radio)*.
*Why it matters:* Reopening Elegu restores vital land transport for trade with East Africa, while advancing studies on Grand Fulla addresses South Sudan's severe long-term power generation deficit.

### Local Governments Crack Down on Illegal Alcohol and Regulatory Violations
Juba City Council Caretaker Mayor Christopher Serafino Wani Swaka issued Local Order No. 04 of 2026, establishing an SSP 10 million fine for establishments selling alcohol to minors *(Sudans Post)*. In Western Equatoria, Mundri West Caretaker Commissioner Charles January Clement reported the seizure of a truckload of alcohol and suspected illegal drugs *(Eye Radio)*.
*Why it matters:* Local administrations are increasingly resorting to economic penalties and confiscations to address public order and health concerns in urban and rural centers.

### Ministry of Justice Appoints 165 Legal Officers
Justice Minister Dr. Wek Mamer Kuol and the ministry's undersecretary signed orders appointing 165 lawyers to legal positions, including 35 Second Legal Counsels and 130 Assistant Legal Counsels *(Eye Radio)*.
*Why it matters:* Expanding the state's legal cadre is necessary to build institutional capacity, handle commercial disputes, and staff judicial administration across the country.
