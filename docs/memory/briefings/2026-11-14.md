## 2. Dinka Heritage Lesson: The Social Structure of Age-Sets (*Ric*)
In Dinka (Muonyjang) society, social identity and civic responsibility outside the immediate clan are anchored in the age-set system, known as *Ric* (or *Rik*). When young men reach late adolescence, they undergo traditional initiation together, forming a named age-set that binds them as peers for life.

Members of a *Ric* share deep obligations of solidarity, mutual aid, and collective defense. They eat together, assist one another during economic hardship, and arbitrate disputes within their group. Historically, age-sets formed the primary structure for community defense and public works. For anyone looking to understand leadership and community dynamics in South Sudan today, recognizing the influence of *Ric* is essential, as respect, trust, and social influence frequently flow through these lifelong peer networks.

  * *What happened:* Clashes between rival sections in Tonj East County over land and cattle left at least four people dead. In Aweil East County, a local administrator and his deputy were shot dead *(Eye Radio)*.

  * *What happened:* Media and civil society groups—including UJOSS, CEPO, and the Female Journalists Network—condemned the ambush in Mundri East that killed veteran journalist Veronica Lucy Gordon and another delegate *(Sudans Post, Eye Radio)*.
  * *Why it matters:* The attack underscores severe security threats facing media personnel, civic leaders, and political candidates traveling along regional transport corridors.
* **Former IGP Appointed Interior Undersecretary**
  * *What happened:* General Atem Marol Biar, former Inspector General of Police, was officially appointed Undersecretary in the Ministry of Interior *(Eye Radio)*.
  * *Why it matters:* Placing a senior law enforcement officer in executive ministry leadership indicates a push to tighten internal security oversight.
* **Juba Prepares Enforcement on Waste Disposal**
  * *What happened:* Juba Caretaker Mayor Christopher Sarafino Wani Swaka set a December deadline to transition from public awareness to active law enforcement against illegal garbage dumping *(Sudans Post)*.
  * *Why it matters:* Commercial operators and property owners in Juba will need to audit their waste disposal systems to avoid fines as municipal authorities enforce sanitation codes.
