* First seen: 2026-11-11

* What to watch: Whether technical preparations proceed toward December polls and whether high-level security/dispute mechanisms are established.

### Regional Yellow Fever & Health Screening Protocols
* First seen: 2026-11-11
* Latest status (2026-11-11): NPHI confirmed a fatal yellow fever case (48-year-old male) in Morobo County, Central Equatoria. CDC extended entry restrictions for 30 days, while Elegu border post with Uganda reopened under 21-day Ebola screening rules.
* What to watch: Public health tracking along Central Equatoria trade corridors near Uganda and DRC.

### Mobile Internet Data Tariff Hike & Telecom FX Access
* First seen: 2026-11-11
* Latest status (2026-11-11): Parliament plans to summon National Communication Authority over a proposed 100% data tariff increase. Telecoms seek direct Central Bank FX reserve access while government plans market interventions to strengthen the SSP.
* What to watch: Parliamentary response to data tariff hike and central bank FX allocation decisions.

### Oil Exploration Push Ahead of SSOP 2026

* Latest status (2026-11-11): South Sudan is marketing 14 oil blocks to reach 230,000 bpd ahead of South Sudan Oil & Power 2026. SANPC joined as a sponsor and is advancing exploration on Block B2.
* What to watch: New commercial exploration commitments during SSOP 2026.
