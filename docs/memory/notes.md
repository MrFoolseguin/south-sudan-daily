## Ongoing storylines

### General Elections and Voter Registration
* **First seen:** 2026-11-15
* **Latest status (2026-11-15):** Voter registration officially launched nationwide on Nov 14, 2026. The National Elections Commission set general election polling day for Feb 11, 2027, with campaigns scheduled for Dec 27, 2026 – Feb 9, 2027.
* **What to watch:** Turnout rates, field readiness in remote counties, and security during voter registration.

### Wau County Civilian Disarmament
* **First seen:** 2026-11-15
* **Latest status (2026-11-15):** The SSPDF Fifth Infantry Division launched a six-month civilian disarmament operation in Wau County, Western Bahr el Ghazal State, aimed at retrieving unauthorized firearms.
* **What to watch:** Conduct of forces, weapons recovery metrics, and security conditions along regional trade routes.

### Commercial Court Establishment in Juba
* **First seen:** 2026-11-15
* **Latest status (2026-11-15):** The Judiciary established a specialized Commercial Court in Juba to resolve corporate transactions, company registrations, and business disputes.
* **What to watch:** Jurisdictional procedures, case turnaround times, and impact on private investor confidence.

### Relocation of University of Upper Nile
* **First seen:** 2026-11-15
* **Latest status (2026-11-15):** Reopened in Malakal by Caretaker Governor James Koang Chuol Ranley, ending 13 years of temporary operation in Juba.
* **What to watch:** Campus infrastructure readiness and local human capital development in Upper Nile State.

### Transit Security in Western Equatoria
* **First seen:** 2026-11-15
* **Latest status (2026-11-15):** Unidentified gunmen ambushed SPLM delegates and killed veteran journalist Veronica Lucy Gordon along the Mundri East–Mundri West transit corridor.
* **What to watch:** Military responses, security for election workers, and safety of commercial movement.

### South Sudan–Sudan Oil Sector Cooperation
* **First seen:** 2026-11-15
* **Latest status (2026-11-15):** South Sudan and Sudan signed an MoU in Khartoum regarding crude oil reconciliation, pipeline audits, and cleanup. Kon John Akot was appointed Petroleum Undersecretary.
* **What to watch:** Execution of joint crude audits and cross-border pipeline maintenance.

### Juba Municipal Sanitation Regulations
* **First seen:** 2026-11-15
* **Latest status (2026-11-15):** Juba City Council announced that active law enforcement and fines for illegal garbage dumping will begin in December 2026.
* **What to watch:** Business compliance, enforcement actions, and growth in private waste management services.
