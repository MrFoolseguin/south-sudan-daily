## Ongoing storylines

### General Elections Preparation
- **First seen:** 2026-11-03
- **Latest status (2026-11-03):** National Elections Commission delivered the first consignment of voter registration materials (books, supplementary forms, laminating equipment) to Central Equatoria State High Elections Committee ahead of planned Dec 22 national elections. (Sudans Post, Eye Radio)
- **What to watch:** Rollout of voter registration materials to remaining states and administrative readiness.

### Juba Municipal Revenue and Regulatory Enforcement
- **First seen:** 2026-11-03
- **Latest status (2026-11-03):** Juba City Council terminated 32 employees over internal fraud that leaked ~50% of tax collections, and ordered the immediate closure of a non-compliant water facility in Kator. (Sudans Post, Eye Radio)
- **What to watch:** Tax collection enforcement, municipal cleanup steps, and impacts on local business compliance.

### Russia-South Sudan Strategic Cooperation
- **First seen:** 2026-11-03
- **Latest status (2026-11-03):** Council of Ministers endorsed strategic cooperation with Russia in petroleum, mining, and IT, following an intergovernmental agreement establishing a Joint Intergovernmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific, and Technical Cooperation. (Eye Radio, Russian MFA)
- **What to watch:** Implementation of joint commission initiatives and economic ties.

### Foreign Debt Recovery and Oil Asset Arbitration
- **First seen:** 2026-11-03
- **Latest status (2026-11-03):** Qatar National Bank is attempting to recover $1 billion from South Sudan following a court victory, while a UAE-based investor launched international arbitration over disputed oil assets. (Africa Intelligence, Eye Radio)
- **What to watch:** Government response to international legal claims and court judgements.

### SPLM-IO Leadership and Court Proceedings
- **First seen:** 2026-11-03
- **Latest status (2026-11-03):** Dr. Riek Machar appeared before the Special Court in Juba, confirming Regina Joseph Kaba as acting chairperson in his absence and denying command over White Army youth militia. (Sudans Post)
- **What to watch:** Ongoing judicial proceedings and internal movement dynamics.

### Inter-State Pastoralist Relocation
- **First seen:** 2026-11-03
- **Latest status (2026-11-03):** Western and Central Equatoria states indicated openness to negotiate with Lakes State regarding temporary relocation of pastoralists fleeing drought, requiring strict pre-arranged agreements. (Eye Radio)
- **What to watch:** Outcome of inter-state talks and protection agreements for farming communities.

### South Sudan-Uganda Border Clashes
- **First seen:** 2026-11-03
- **Latest status (2026-11-03):** Clashes involving South Sudanese rebel elements spilled across the Ugandan border, resulting in two deaths (including a Ugandan civilian woman) and three injured. (Eye Radio, Uganda Observer)
- **What to watch:** Joint border security enforcement and diplomatic contact between Juba and Kampala.

### Government Support for Crawford Capital
- **First seen:** 2026-11-03
- **Latest status (2026-11-03):** Ministry of ICT and Postal Services publicly expressed support for Crawford Capital despite recent measures imposed on the company by the United States government. (Eye Radio)
- **What to watch:** US regulatory actions and potential diplomatic pushback.
