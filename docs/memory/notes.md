## Ongoing storylines

### 2027 General Elections Calendar & Rules
- **First-seen:** 2026-11-16
- **Latest status (2026-11-16):** National Elections Commission confirmed polling day for February 11, 2027. SPLM primaries enforced SSPDF resignation rules, overturning Yel Deng Nguel's win in Aweil East (naming runner-up Kom Kom Geng), while Epone Emmanuel Lolimo won the primary in Kapoeta North.
- **What to watch:** Enforcement of military resignation rules, voter registration progress, and base mobilization leading to 2027.

### Primary Examination Access and Political Exclusions
- **First-seen:** 2026-11-16
- **Latest status (2026-11-16):** 87,892 students began CPE exams, but 534 were excluded, including 393 in Panyijiar County due to cancelled centers in opposition zones, prompting local appeals to separate politics from basic education.
- **What to watch:** National government response to appeals and impact on local resentment in opposition areas.

### Economic Growth and Food Import Dependency
- **First-seen:** 2026-11-16
- **Latest status (2026-11-16):** South Sudan is projected to grow 19% in 2026 on oil recovery, but faces a $800M annual food import bill. The government announced plans for a domestic gold refinery.
- **What to watch:** Execution of gold refinery plans and local agricultural import substitution projects.

### Ugandan Traders Strike and Trade Corridor Disruptions
- **First-seen:** 2026-11-16
- **Latest status (2026-11-16):** Kampala City Traders Association (KACITA) announced a month-long nationwide strike starting November 17 over taxes and customs procedures.
- **What to watch:** Transit delays at the Nimule border and rising food/commodity prices in Juba markets.

### Women's Customary Land Reform
- **First-seen:** 2026-11-16
- **Latest status (2026-11-16):** IGAD secured commitments from local communities in Eastern Equatoria State to reform customary rules restricting women's land ownership.
- **What to watch:** Formal adoption of tenure reforms by traditional chieftaincies and county land offices.
