## Ongoing storylines

* **Free Political Movement & Security Roadblocks Order**
  * *First seen:* 2026-11-26
  * *Latest status (2026-11-26):* President Salva Kiir issued Republican Orders instructing security personnel to remove illegal roadblocks, end unauthorized tax collection on humanitarian/diplomatic transport, and guarantee free movement for political candidates ahead of the 2027 elections *(Sudans Post, Eye Radio)*. Opposition parties are calling for strict security neutrality.
  * *What to watch:* Security force compliance, opposition party security guarantees, and effects on aid/commerce transit costs.

* **2027 Election Voter Registration Deadline**
  * *First seen:* 2026-11-26
  * *Latest status (2026-11-26):* Civil society groups officially petitioned the National Elections Commission to extend voter registration beyond the November 28 cutoff *(Eye Radio)*.
  * *What to watch:* Whether the National Elections Commission grants an extension before November 28.

* **SPLM Internal Primaries for 2027 Elections**
  * *First seen:* 2026-11-26
  * *Latest status (2026-11-26):* Agok Manyiel Dhieu secured the SPLM nomination for Lakes State governor by five votes over Madhang Majok Meen, while Ambassador Malek Ruben won the nomination for Rumbek East parliamentary constituency *(Sudans Post, YouTube)*.
  * *What to watch:* Further primary results and internal party cohesion ahead of candidate nomination deadlines.

* **National Energy Targets and Asian Oil Partnerships**
  * *First seen:* 2026-11-26
  * *Latest status (2026-11-26):* The Ministry of Petroleum targeted short-term crude production of 200,000 bpd and renewed operational agreements with Chinese and Malaysian partners *(Pan African Visions, Oil & Gas Middle East, Yeni Safak English)*. President Kiir publicly noted oil revenues have not yet improved general living standards *(Radio Tamazuj)*.
  * *What to watch:* Production output progress and plans to reach 50% power grid access by 2035.

* **Bank of South Sudan Forex Crackdown**
  * *First seen:* 2026-11-26
  * *Latest status (2026-11-26):* The Bank of South Sudan warned it will immediately shut down unlicensed foreign exchange operators *(Sudans Post)*.
  * *What to watch:* Scope and timing of regulatory enforcement actions against uncertified dealers.

* **Media Development Institute Operational Resumption**
  * *First seen:* 2026-11-26
  * *Latest status (2026-11-26):* The Media Authority lifted its suspension of the Media Development Institute following instructions from Information Minister Ateny Wek Ateny *(Sudans Post, Eye Radio)*.
  * *What to watch:* Resumption of journalist training programs ahead of election reporting.

* **Riek Machar Court Testimony on Historical Evacuations**
  * *First seen:* 2026-11-26
  * *Latest status (2026-11-26):* Testifying in special court, Dr. Riek Machar denied ordering Maj. Gen. David Majur Dak to surrender, stating he requested helicopters from the President's Office and UN to evacuate 219 personnel in Nasir *(Sudans Post, Eye Radio)*.
  * *What to watch:* Subsequent judicial proceedings and testimony regarding military command decisions.

* **Sudan Border Instability and Escalation**
  * *First seen:* 2026-11-26
  * *Latest status (2026-11-26):* Fighting and drone strikes between SAF and RSF-aligned forces in Blue Nile State near the border triggered civilian evacuations in Akobo toward Ethiopia, while Sudan announced plans to deploy 6,000 troops along its Ethiopian border *(Addis Standard, Radio Tamazuj, Eye Radio)*.
  * *What to watch:* Cross-border civilian displacement and potential military spillover into South Sudan.
